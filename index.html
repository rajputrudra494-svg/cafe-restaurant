Where it started

Two people, one oven, a town called Bardoli

In 2013 Mr Prashant Patel and his wife came home from Canada with an idea that sounded slightly mad for a small Gujarat town: proper Italian-style pizza, pure vegetarian, no shortcuts. One specialised oven, a short menu, a lot of cheese.

The town turned up. The menu grew to 143 things. Then the pizza moved to Surat as well — same dough, same oven routine, a bigger room with a red steel ceiling.
