Before you ask

Questions weget daily

If yours isn't here, the phone is the fastest way. Someone at the counter will know.

or just turn up, that works too

Is everything really vegetarian?

Yes — the whole menu, both stores, since 2013. The “sausage” and “pepperoni” on the topping list are vegetarian versions.

Can I get it Jain?

A lot of the menu can be made Jain, and those items carry a green J on the menu page. Tell us when you order and the kitchen leaves out what it needs to.

What are your timings?

They move around, so we don't publish them here rather than have you drive over on bad information. Call 95372 46200 or 89809 18008 and we'll tell you what's happening today.

Do you deliver?

Ring the store you want to order from and ask — it depends on where you are and how busy the oven is. You can also send the order on WhatsApp and we'll come back to you.

How big are the pizzas?

Four sizes: 10″ medium, 12″ large, 14″ extra large and 16″ jumbo, each cut into eight. A 16″ is genuinely a table's worth.

Can you do a large order for a party?

Yes, but give us some notice — call ahead with the number of pizzas and when you need them, and we'll plan the oven around it.

Know what you want already?

Send it over and we'll have it ready when you get here.
